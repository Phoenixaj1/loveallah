# Play Store screenshots — capture guide

Google Play requires 2 minimum (8 recommended) **phone** screenshots.

## Specs
- **Format**: PNG or JPEG
- **Min dimension**: 320px on each side
- **Max dimension**: 3840px
- **Aspect ratio**: between 9:16 (portrait) and 16:9 (landscape). Portrait recommended for an Islamic content app.
- **Recommended**: 1080 × 1920 (standard portrait phone)

## How to capture from your phone (easiest)

1. On your Android phone, open https://loveallah.app in Chrome
2. Long-press the URL bar → "Install" or "Add to Home screen" (installs as PWA — gives you the chrome-free TWA-like experience)
3. Open the PWA from your home screen
4. Take screenshots of these surfaces in order:

| # | Surface | What it shows |
|---|---------|---------------|
| 1 | **Home / Feed** — first dhikr card | "Lā ilāha illa-llāh" Arabic dominates, prayer strip above |
| 2 | **Sacred-pause moment** — after completing the 5th dhikr | Deep night background with rotating ayah |
| 3 | **Feed — first video** | Mishary Alafasy or Mufti Menk video playing, scholar attribution visible |
| 4 | **Masjid tab** | Central Jamia Masjid Birmingham + prayer times grid + services |
| 5 | **Connect tab — patron tiers** | Supporter / Guardian / Champion / Visionary |
| 6 | **Saved tab** | Bookmarks library (after saving a couple) |
| 7 | **Hijri date + streak in header** | Close-up of header strip showing 6 Dhu'l-H + 1-day streak |
| 8 | **Bottom nav** | All 6 tabs visible (Feed/Dhikr/Nasheed/Mindful/Masjid/Connect) |

## How to capture from Chrome DevTools (desktop)

1. Open https://loveallah.app in Chrome
2. Press F12 to open DevTools
3. Click the device-toggle icon (Ctrl+Shift+M)
4. Top bar — select "iPhone 14 Pro Max" or "Pixel 7" (any portrait phone)
5. Click the three-dot menu in the device toolbar → "Capture full size screenshot" (or just regular screenshot)
6. Repeat for each surface above
