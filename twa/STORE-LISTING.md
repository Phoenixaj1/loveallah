## Full description (max 4000 chars)
**Love Allah** is a sacred ritual app whose only purpose is to draw each Muslim closer to Allah swt.

The masjid is your pathway. Daily remembrance is your rhythm. The feed feeds your heart.

### What's inside

🤲 **Daily affirmation dhikr** — five short remembrance moments interleaved into your feed every day. Lā ilāha illa-llāh. Alḥamdulillāh. Astaghfirullāh. Subḥānallāh. Allāhu akbar.

🎵 **Curated nasheeds** — Sami Yusuf, Maher Zain, and a growing library of soul-stirring vocal music from artists we trust.

📿 **Dhikr loops & Qira'at** — Mishary Rashed Alafasy, Ahmed Bukhatir and other reciters whose voices carry the Qur'an straight to the heart.

🌿 **Mindfulness for the believer** — ambient Islamic sounds, du'a meditations and reflective moments for focus or rest.

🕌 **Your masjid, in your pocket** — connect to your local masjid and get prayer times in your area, jamaat schedules, events, services and announcements.

🤝 **Connect with the ummah** — find Muslim businesses and vetted services from people in your community. Become a patron to unlock features and support the cause.

### Where your support goes

Love Allah is funded by ads and patronage. **Every penny flows through YourNiyyah and is split between** verified Islamic charities, charitable Islamic projects, and the masjids that join our network. When you support Love Allah, you support the ummah.

### Built with care

- Portrait-first like the apps you already love
- Sacred design — no arcade gamification, just gentle stillness
- Privacy-respecting — no third-party trackers, no behavioural ad networks
- Built by Muslims, for Muslims

May Allah swt accept it from us and from you.
